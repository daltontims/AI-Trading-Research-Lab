# H4-06 — BTC funding-rate contrarian carry

*Pre-registered 2026-11-17 before any test was run.*

**Question.** When perpetual funding is very high (crowded longs), does shorting earn funding plus a price reversal?

**Rationale.** High funding means leveraged longs pay shorts; crowded positioning may unwind.

**Rules.** `lab.strategies.phase4.FundingContrarian` — Funding-rate contrarian/carry: short when longs pay high funding, long when shorts pay.
Fixed parameters: `{"exit_threshold": 0.0001}`. Parameter grid: `{"threshold": [0.0003, 0.0005, 0.001]}`.

**Data.** BTCUSDT, 1h bars, development split only for the search.
**Costs.** profile `crypto_perp`, with funding. **Sizing/risk.** `{"sizing": "pct_equity", "position_pct": 1.0}`; $5,000 start, no leverage.

**Success criteria.** All gates in configs/protocol.yaml (screen at 2× costs, Deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95, PBO ≤ 0.2, SPA p ≤ 0.05, walk-forward, parameter plateau, yearly consistency), then one validation look.
