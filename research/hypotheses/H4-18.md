# H4-18 — SPY turn-of-the-month

*Pre-registered 2026-11-17 before any test was run.*

**Question.** Does SPY earn most of its return from the last trading day of a month to the first 2–4 days of the next?

**Rationale.** Month-end pension and payroll flows; one of the oldest calendar anomalies.

**Rules.** `lab.strategies.phase4.TurnOfMonth` — Turn-of-the-month: hold from the last trading day of the month for the first n trading days.
Fixed parameters: `{}`. Parameter grid: `{"days_after": [2, 3, 4]}`.

**Data.** SPY, 1d bars, development split only for the search.
**Costs.** profile `default`. **Sizing/risk.** `{"sizing": "pct_equity", "position_pct": 1.0}`; $5,000 start, no leverage.

**Success criteria.** All gates in configs/protocol.yaml (screen at 2× costs, Deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95, PBO ≤ 0.2, SPA p ≤ 0.05, walk-forward, parameter plateau, yearly consistency), then one validation look.
