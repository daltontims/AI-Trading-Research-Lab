**Rationale.** Avoiding long bear markets; one of the most cited timing rules.

**Rules.** `lab.strategies.phase4.TrendFilter` — Price above its n-bar moving average -> long (or short below, if allowed).
Fixed parameters: `{}`. Parameter grid: `{"n": [50, 100, 150, 200, 250]}`.

**Costs.** profile `default`. **Sizing/risk.** `{"sizing": "pct_equity", "position_pct": 1.0}`; $5,000 start, no leverage.
